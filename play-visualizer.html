Base court positionsMove players to explore spacing and coverage. Choose a preset or play an example rally to see how the ball travels.

HOW TO USE

  1. Choose a formation preset.
  2. Drag a blue circle to move a player.
  3. Tap Rotate to advance one position.
  4. Tap Play rally to follow ball movement.
  5. Use Reset to start again.

Teaching note: formations are simplified examples. Your coach's assignments, overlap rules and game situation determine where players should actually stand.
